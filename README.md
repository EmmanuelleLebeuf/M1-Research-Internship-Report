#  Master's Research Internship Report: Egalitarian Allocation Rules in Network Games

> **Supervised by:** Mr. Sylvain Béal and Mr. Kevin Techer
> **Period:** March 2026 – June 2026

##  Abstract & Core Contributions

This research work investigates cost and surplus-sharing problems within cooperative network games, bridging the gap between individual strategic positioning and local network architectures. The study axiomatically characterizes and develops three distinct allocation rules:

*  **The $Y^{ER}$ Rule (Network EANC):** Adapts the classical Equal Allocation of Non-Separable Cost to graph structures, combining marginal contributions with an egalitarian distribution of global surplus. It is uniquely characterized by global efficiency and Collective Balanced Contributions (CCER).
*  **The NESD Rule (Neighborhood Equal Surplus Division):** Shifts the perspective from global topology to immediate local neighborhoods. It relies on the *Fairness under Neighborhood Restriction (FNR)* axiom—reflecting economic embeddedness—to ensure that direct neighbors fairly share local gains or losses.
*  **The WNESD Rule (Weighted Neighborhood Equal Surplus Division):** Generalizes the local approach by introducing a weighted mechanism based on agents' connectivity degrees, ensuring that network centrality directly influences final retribution.
